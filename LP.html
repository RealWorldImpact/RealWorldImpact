Buy with ETH and receive ETH when you sell. Every trade on this page routes through RWI: ETH to RWI to token, or token to RWI to ETH. The launch trading fee, creator tax, and ETH/RWI pool fees apply.

Your token starts on a bonding curve with the same pricing formula and graduation ratio as Pons v2. Buyers supply the RWI, so you need no RWI to launch.

Once the curve sells its allocation, the collected RWI and reserved tokens seed full-range Uniswap v4 liquidity. The pool keeps the curve’s final price. Liquidity and the remaining token reserve are permanently locked.

Every launch targets a $5,000 starting market cap using a fresh RWI USD price. The RWI amounts are fixed at launch; their dollar values move with RWI. Creators can claim earned fees in ETH, but cannot withdraw liquidity, raise the tax, or mint more tokens.

A launch can lose value or attract no buyers. The starting valuation does not guarantee demand or returns.
