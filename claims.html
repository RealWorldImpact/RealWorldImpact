How the number is built

For every transaction where a submitted wallet's v1 balance changed, the counter-value on the other side of that same transaction is measured: ETH sent, ETH returned internally, and any transfer of a token priced below.

net loss = spent − received − value still held

Acquisitions add to spent. Disposals add to received. Transfers with no counter-value — airdrops, wallet-to-wallet sends, liquidity moves — are counted at zero and flagged, because the chain alone cannot tell what they were worth to you.

ETH is valued at the rate the explorer recorded for that block where available, otherwise at the current rate. Results are cached in your browser and can be recomputed from scratch at any time.

Prices used for valuation (USD)

v1 is retired and priced at zero by default, so anything a wallet still holds counts fully as loss. Change either figure and the register recalculates.

Quote assets recognised

Submissions are public. Anyone can enter any address, so an entry records interest in a wallet, not ownership of it. This register reports what the chain shows; it is not a promise of repayment, and it is not financial or tax advice.
