Graduation is based on the token reaching at least a $40,000 valuation and having at least $5,000 of actual pool liquidity. There is no fixed RWI token-count target or per-launch RWI cap. RWI quantities vary with its verified live price.

The local V4 candidate implements these dollar-based rules. The LP interface and discovery feeds are integrated and tested locally. Deployment still needs the independent RWI/USD provider selected by the project.

Valuation here means price × the full billion-token supply. Waiting for minimum liquidity can mean graduation happens above $40,000. Future price growth is a scenario to test, not an assumption required for safety.
