The dollar thresholds stay fixed. The number of RWI tokens needed changes with its price. At the checked example price, $2,500 is about 91.6 million RWI; at 10× that price, it is about 9.16 million RWI. Fees and the cost of moving the market are additional.

The earlier 1% reserve cap cannot support the selected $5,000 pool minimum at the checked price. A reliable live RWI conversion price and revised reserve rules must be implemented and verified before a replacement can be deployed.

Valuation here means price × the full billion-token supply. Waiting for minimum liquidity can mean graduation happens above $50,000. Future price growth is a scenario to test, not an assumption required for safety.
