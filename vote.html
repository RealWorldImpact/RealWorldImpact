Community signal

You hold it.
You pick who's next.

$RWI holders signal which vetted charity should receive the next donation. Your vote is a transaction on Robinhood Chain, weighted by the $RWI you hold. Anyone can tally it — including you.

Read this first This is an advisory signal, not binding governance. $RWI grants no governance rights and no claim on revenue. The project reads the result and weighs it, but retains final say over every donation — recipients must clear The Giving Block's vetting regardless of how the vote lands. Voting is weighted by token balance, which means larger holders carry more weight. Balances are read live at tally time, so a vote is not locked to a snapshot.

The page reads every transaction ever sent to that address, decodes the choices, and keeps the most recent vote per wallet.

03

Each wallet's weight is its live $RWI balance, read from the token contract. Wallets holding nothing carry no weight.
