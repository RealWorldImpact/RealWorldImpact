Pricing and liquidity

Swap amounts reflect actual RWI spent or received, with the curve fee reported separately. Display execution prices from those amounts. The marginal curve price before fees is (phantom RWI + real RWI reserve) / curve token inventory.

Reported reserves contain only accounted curve tokens and real RWI. Phantom RWI, fee rewards, unsolicited transfers and unused supply are excluded. The ratio of real reserves alone is not the curve price. There is no fabricated initial liquidity or trade volume.

Graduation occurs when the fixed sale allocation is bought out. The $5,000 start, $40,000 graduation valuation and $5,000 graduated pool are opening estimates, not guaranteed future dollar values.

Items for DEXTools review

  • Venue onboarding: DEXTools must accept and enable this custom curve integration before its tokens can appear there.
  • Holder distribution: /asset/holders returns 501. DEXTools can use its EVM holder index, or agree on an exact fractional-balance format. Holder counts are not fabricated.
  • Pricing: confirm support for execution prices and virtual curve pricing without treating phantom reserves as funded liquidity.
  • Graduation: pair metadata and the rwiMigrations extension identify the actual graduated v4 pool. DEXTools must agree on the chart handoff; the adapter does not invent join or exit events.
  • Hosting: this adapter uses public RPC history and bounded in-memory caches. Cold replay is limited to 6.4 million blocks and 10,000 events per token. Larger histories require a persistent index. Incomplete or unverifiable responses fail instead of returning partial data.
